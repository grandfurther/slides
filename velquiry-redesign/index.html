How healthy is your digital presence?

Velquiry helps founders make better business decisions through evidence-led website, growth, and operating systems. Let’s find out.

Case files / Open investigations

Four characters who will sell you a fix you don’t need

If one is on your payroll, ask the question at the bottom of their file.
FILE 001OPEN

The retainer bandit

Method
A 12-month contract and a monthly report full of activity. Results are always one quarter away.
Evidence to request
Customers or qualified enquiries per month since the contract started.

Ask them“What happens if I cancel in month three?”

FILE 002OPEN

The vanity metric peddler

Method
Reach, impressions, and engagement in large green numbers. Revenue never makes the slide.
Evidence to request
The list of enquiries the campaign produced, with names.

Ask them“What did each paying customer cost?”

FILE 003OPEN

The made-up-number merchant

Method
A “free audit” with a score of 43/100 before anyone has opened your site. Every finding is urgent; none are shown.
Evidence to request
The data behind each score, and which checks failed to run.

Ask them“Show me how you got that number.”

FILE 004OPEN

The tool pusher

Method
Every problem needs new software, and the recommended software pays the recommender a commission.
Evidence to request
The problem the tool solves, stated in your own numbers.

Ask them“Do you get paid if I buy this?”

Dear Velquiry

Questions from founders. Names and firms withheld.

Our agency says the ads are “performing well.” Our phone hasn’t rung more than usual in four months. Who do I believe?

— Founder, home services, Singapore

THE EDITOR: Believe the phone. Ask for one number: how many paying customers came from the ads last month, and what each one cost. If they can’t produce it, they’re reporting on the ads, not on your business.

Someone told me I need an AI chatbot on my website. Do I?

— Founder, clinic, Tampines

THE EDITOR: Maybe later. First send a test enquiry through your own form and time the reply. If a human answers within the hour, a chatbot adds little. If nobody answers for two days, fix that first.

Your plan builder came out above our budget. Is that the end of it?

— Co-founder, retail brand, Singapore

THE EDITOR: No. Most plans can be phased. Start with the one system your scorecard flags first, and add the rest when it pays for itself.
