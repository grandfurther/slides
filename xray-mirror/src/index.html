Use it on a call

  1. Quickest: press H to hide these controls, then share this window or tab in Zoom, Meet or Teams.
  2. As your camera: install OBS Studio, add a Window Capture of this window, click Start Virtual Camera, then pick “OBS Virtual Camera” as the camera in your call app.
  3. On Windows, turn your camera off in the call app first, since two apps often can’t share one webcam.
